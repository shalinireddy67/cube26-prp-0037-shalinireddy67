What it checks

Built around the three things that get FBA shipments rejected.

Barcode visibility detection

Confirms your FNSKU or UPC barcode is unobstructed, unfolded, and scannable before it reaches a fulfillment center. Tape, labels, or box seams over the code are flagged before you ship.

Packing quality check

Flags loose tape, missing poly bags, crushed corners, and other prep issues that trigger Amazon rejections.

Compliance report

A downloadable report you can keep for your prep records or hand to your packing team.
